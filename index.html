Kalpana Chawla

Dreamer • Astronaut • Inspiration

A Woman Who Reached the Stars

Kalpana Chawla was an Indian-born astronaut and aerospace engineer who became an inspiration for millions of people.

"The path from dreams to success does exist."

About Kalpana Chawla

Kalpana Chawla was born on 17 March 1962 in Karnal, Haryana. She developed an interest in flying and space from a young age. Her dedication and hard work helped her achieve her dream of becoming an astronaut.

Major Achievements

🚀 First Space Mission

She travelled to space for the first time in 1997 aboard Space Shuttle Columbia.

🌎 NASA Astronaut

She became a NASA astronaut and worked in the field of aerospace and space exploration.

⭐ Inspiration

Her journey continues to inspire students to dream big and work hard.

Her Journey

1962 — Born in Karnal, Haryana.

1982 — Completed her Bachelor's degree in Aeronautical Engineering.

1997 — Travelled to space for the first time.

2003 — Became part of the Columbia mission.

Her Legacy

Kalpana Chawla proved that dreams can become reality through courage, dedication and hard work. Her story will continue to inspire future generations.
